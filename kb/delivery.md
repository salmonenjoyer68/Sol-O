# Weekend delivery

We deliver on Saturdays and Sundays. Weekend orders placed before 14:00 local time are dispatched the same day; later orders go out on the next delivery day.

Weekend delivery costs the same as a weekday delivery: 4.99 flat, and free above 50.

# Pricing

The standard range starts at 12 for the small box and 19 for the family box.

Orders of ten boxes or more get a 10 percent discount.
